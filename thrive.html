From zero to a budget

Your first budget, in about fifteen minutes

No sign-up, no account, and nothing to connect. Thrive walks you through this the first time you open it — here is the whole thing, start to finish.

  1. 1

    Export a CSV from your bank

    Log in to your bank, choose “Export” or “Download transactions,” and pick CSV. One account is enough to start. That is the entire connection — no bank linking, ever, and no password to hand over.

  2. 2

    Drop it into Thrive

    Thrive reads the file and sorts the spending into categories on the way in. Most of it is already tagged before you look — you are reviewing, not typing.

  3. 3

    Connect your email

    Let Thrive read the transaction alerts your bank already emails you, so spending stays current all month instead of only when you remember to export. Works with Gmail, iCloud, Outlook, Yahoo, Fastmail, AOL — any IMAP inbox. This is what keeps the whole thing effortless: without it you are back to exporting a CSV by hand every month.

  4. 4

    Make the categories yours

    Rename, move, or add categories and the parent groups that hold them. Every merchant you tag is remembered, so the same store sorts itself next time. A few minutes here is what makes every later month automatic.

  5. 5

    Get your first budget — and share it

    Thrive drafts a zero-based budget from your real numbers, so every dollar of income has a job and the month balances to zero. Invite your spouse and you are both looking at the same plan — two Apple IDs, no shared password.

After that it mostly runs itself. New spending arrives categorized from your email scan, recurring bills are set aside automatically, and the budget rolls forward each month — you adjust rather than rebuild.

Our approach to your data

This is the foundation of every tool we create.

Your data stays with you

Your financial information lives on your devices and syncs only through your own private iCloud — Apple's encrypted database that we cannot read. There's no Beacon server: we never receive, store, or have access to any of it.

We do not ask for your data

You do not need to create an account with us. You do not need to upload your information. Optional features such as local import keep everything under your control on your device.

No tracking of any kind

Our tools do not track your activity, build profiles, or send data back to us. This is a deliberate design choice, not a setting you have to enable.

Built for households

Household Sharing is here: invite your spouse to the same budget from their own Apple ID, peer-to-peer through iCloud. Same view, same numbers, no shared login — and it follows the same rule as everything else: we never see your data.

You bring your own records

Import files from your own sources when you want to. We have no visibility into the data you choose to work with.

Protection by design

We build this way because we believe in looking after the people who use our tools. Your data is yours. We treat it that way.

Common questions

Is Thrive really free?

Yes. The core zero-based budgeting toolkit — budgets, transactions, imports, dashboard — is free forever, with no account and no ads. Premium ($8/mo) adds retirement, education, and real-estate planning tools, with a 7-day free trial in-app.

Does Thrive connect to my bank?

No — by design. You never hand over bank credentials. Import transactions from the CSV, PDF, or OFX files your bank already gives you, or connect your inbox and let Thrive read the transaction-alert emails your bank already sends you. Nothing about your accounts ever touches our servers, because we don't have any.

Where is my financial data stored?

On your device, and synced between your Mac and iPhone through your own private iCloud (Apple's encrypted CloudKit). There is no company server, no account with us, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

What does "zero-based budgeting" mean?

Each dollar of income gets assigned a job — giving, bills, savings, spending — until income minus assignments equals zero. It's the proven method behind envelope budgeting, and it's the fastest way to know exactly where your money goes.

How is Thrive different from other budgeting apps?

Four things. Privacy: most budgeting apps link your bank and hold your finances on their servers — Thrive never does either; your data stays on your Apple devices and your private iCloud, and our App Privacy label reads "Data Not Collected." Family: Household Sharing gives you and your spouse one budget from separate Apple IDs — no shared login, and joining is free. Native: a real Mac app and iPhone app, not a website in a wrapper. Stewardship: Giving/Tithe is a first-class category, and the tools treat money as something you manage faithfully, not just optimize.

Can my spouse and I share one budget?

Yes — Household Sharing is built in. Create a household in Settings, send your spouse an invite link, and they join from their own Apple ID. You both see and edit the same budget, synced peer-to-peer through iCloud — no shared password, no separate account, and nothing passes through our servers. Sharing is included with Premium; joining a household is always free for your spouse.

Is Thrive a Christian budgeting app?

Thrive is built around Christian stewardship — being faithful with what you've been given. Giving comes first in the default budget, and the monthly review is encouraging rather than shaming. That said, it's a full-featured budgeting app anyone can use.
