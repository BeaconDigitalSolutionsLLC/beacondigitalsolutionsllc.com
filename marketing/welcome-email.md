# Buttondown welcome email — Thrive list

Replaces the old AI-prompts welcome. Paste into Buttondown →
**Settings → Emails → Welcome email**.

Written to be read on a phone in under a minute. No images, no tracking
pixel, no "we're excited to have you on board." The list promise is
concrete and small, because a promise you keep monthly beats one you
abandon in six weeks.

**Before sending, set these in Buttondown:** turn OFF open tracking and
click tracking (Settings → Sending). A newsletter from a privacy-first
company should not report back when you opened it — and saying so in the
email only works if it's true.

---

## Subject line

> You're in — here's what you'll get

Alternatives if you want to A/B later:
- `Every dollar gets a job`
- `Welcome to Thrive`

## Preview text

> One email a month. No tracking pixel. Unsubscribe whenever.

---

## Body

Hi — Taylor here. I build Thrive.

Thanks for signing up. Here's exactly what you're agreeing to, so there
are no surprises:

**One email a month.** What shipped in the app, and one practical idea
about handling money well. That's it. No drip sequence, no "limited time"
countdowns, no selling your address to anyone, ever.

**This email doesn't track you.** No pixel telling me you opened it, no
wrapped links reporting what you clicked. I'd rather not know. The
unsubscribe link at the bottom works on the first click and removes your
address entirely.

---

**What Thrive is, in one paragraph**

A budgeting app for Mac and iPhone where every dollar of income gets
assigned a job before the month starts — and where giving comes first,
not last. It never asks for your bank login. Your records sync through
your own private iCloud, so there's no server of mine holding your
financial life. Go check the App Store privacy label; it reads *Data Not
Collected.*

The free version is genuinely free — budgets, transactions, imports, the
dashboard. Premium ($8/month, 7-day trial) adds retirement and college
projections, real estate and mortgage payoff, and the year-to-date
review.

→ **Get Thrive:** https://apps.apple.com/app/id6779286706

---

**One thing worth trying this week**

Before you spend anything next month, write down what your income is
going to do — every dollar of it, including the part that goes to
savings. Most budgets fail not because people overspend, but because a
few hundred dollars was never assigned anywhere, so it quietly
disappeared. Naming the job is most of the work.

Thanks for being here early. If you try Thrive and something is wrong or
confusing, reply to this email — it comes straight to me, and early
feedback shapes what I build next.

— Taylor
Beacon Digital Solutions

---

## Notes for later

- **Reply-to matters.** Make sure replies land in an inbox you read; "reply
  to this email" is the highest-value line here and it has to be true.
- **Monthly cadence starts the month after signup.** Don't send a second
  email in the first week — it contradicts the promise you just made.
- **Once you have testimonials**, swap the "one thing worth trying" section
  for a real user's sentence. Nothing converts like someone else's words.
